# Daily Ledger — Budget Passbook

Daily Ledger is a simple app that tells you one thing clearly: **how much you can safely spend today.**

Instead of a monthly budget you have to work out yourself, it takes whatever money you have left, looks at how many days are left in your current pay cycle, and splits it evenly — so you always know your number for the day. It looks and feels like an old-school bank passbook, and it works entirely on your phone. No sign-up, no account needed, no internet required once installed.

## What it does

- **Tells you what's safe to spend today** — one clear number on the home screen, recalculated every time you log something.
- **Follows your actual payday, not the calendar** — most budgeting apps reset on the 1st of the month. This one doesn't. You start a new 30-day cycle whenever you actually get paid, whether that's a fixed date or not.
- **Tracks cash and online money separately** — so you always know how much is in your wallet versus your bank/UPI, plus a combined total.
- **Helps you save toward a goal** — set a savings target for the cycle and watch your progress as you go.
- **Sorts your spending into categories** — Food, Travel, Shopping, Bills, Health, or Other — so you can see where your money actually goes.
- **Keeps a full history** — every entry shows up in a passbook-style ledger you can scroll back through, filterable by cash, online, or both.
- **Comes in different looks** — Dark, Light, Dusk, or Auto (switches automatically depending on the time of day).
- **Works like a real app** — install it on your home screen and it keeps working even without signal or Wi-Fi.
- **Keeps your data safe** — back up as a file, or connect Google Drive so it backs up automatically.

## Getting it onto your phone

### Android
1. Open this link on your phone in **Chrome**: [https://blinding-dash.github.io/Daily-Ledger/](https://blinding-dash.github.io/Daily-Ledger/)
2. Tap the **⋮** (three dots) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
3. Tap **Install app** — or just tap the install banner if Chrome offers one automatically at the bottom.
4. Confirm the install.

You'll now have a "Daily Ledger" icon on your home screen and app drawer, just like any other app. It opens full-screen with no browser bar, and keeps working even without internet.

### iPhone
1. Open the same link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** icon.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.

## Using the app

### First time setup
The first time you open it, it'll ask for three things:
- **Savings goal** — how much you'd like to set aside this cycle (₹0 is fine if you don't want one right now).
- **Cash in hand** — how much physical cash you currently have.
- **Online / bank balance** — how much is in your bank or UPI accounts right now.

That's all it needs to get started.

> **Already have a backup from before?** Don't fill in the setup form — instead, look for **"Restore from Google Drive"** or **"Restore from backup file"** on that same first screen. Filling in the setup form creates a brand-new empty cycle, which can get in the way of restoring your old data.

### Logging money
Two buttons on the home screen: **+ Log income** and **+ Log expense**.

- For an expense, pick a category (Food, Travel, Shopping, Bills, Health, Other), whether it was cash or online, and the amount.
- For income, just enter the amount and where it went (cash or online).

### Starting a new cycle when you get paid
When you log income, you'll see a checkbox: **"Start a new 30-day cycle from today."**

- Leave it **unchecked** if this is just a top-up (a gift, a refund, side income) — it simply adds to your current cycle's budget.
- **Check it** when this is your actual pay/allowance arriving — it closes out the current cycle and starts a fresh 30-day one from today, carrying over any money you haven't spent yet.

This is the key idea behind the app: you decide when your "month" starts, based on your own payday.

### The home screen
- The big ring shows **how much you can spend today** — it shrinks as you log expenses and adjusts automatically each day.
- Below it, your **cash and online balances**.
- A **savings goal bar** showing progress toward what you set aside.
- A list of everything logged **today**.

### History tab
A full passbook of every entry this cycle — income (credit), expenses (debit), and a running balance — plus a breakdown of spending by category. Filter it to see cash-only or online-only if you want.

### Settings tab
- Change your **theme** (Dark, Light, Dusk, or Auto).
- Edit this cycle's **savings goal**, **cash/online balances**, or manually correct the **cycle start date**.
- **Back up and restore** your data (see below).
- **Reset the cycle** if you want to clear all entries and start over — your goal and balances stay put.

## Keeping your data safe (backups)

Your entries are saved on your device automatically, so you don't need to do anything extra day-to-day. But if you ever get a new phone, or want extra peace of mind, there are two easy ways to back things up:

**Option 1: Save a backup file**
Settings → **Download backup**. This saves a file with all your data, which you can keep somewhere safe or bring to a new device with **Restore from backup**.

**Option 2: Connect Google Drive (recommended)**
Settings → **Connect Google Drive**. Once connected, the app quietly keeps a backup of your data in your Drive, so if you ever switch devices or reinstall the app, you can just reconnect and get everything back — no need to remember to do it yourself.

## Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you enter stays on your own device unless you choose to connect Google Drive — and even then, the app only ever touches the one backup file it creates for itself. It can't see or touch anything else in your Drive. There's no tracking, no analytics, and no company collecting your spending data.

## A few things to know

- If you never connect Google Drive or make a manual backup, your data lives only on this device — uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data will erase it.
- This isn't financial advice — it's a simple tool to help you see, day to day, what you can afford to spend.
